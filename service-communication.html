📋 DOT Compliance & Licensing

Navigate complex telecom regulations with our expert compliance and licensing services. We handle all aspects of Department of Telecommunications (DOT) requirements, ensuring your operations meet regulatory standards.

  • OSP (Other Service Provider) license application and renewal
  • ISP (Internet Service Provider) licensing support
  • Unified License consultancy
  • TRAI compliance management
  • Regulatory filing and documentation
  • Compliance audit and gap analysis

📞 Telecom Equipment Solutions

Premium telecommunications equipment from leading manufacturers, installed and configured by certified professionals. We provide end-to-end solutions for voice, data, and unified communications.

  • PBX and IP-PBX systems deployment
  • VoIP gateway and SIP trunk setup
  • Call center infrastructure equipment
  • Video conferencing systems
  • Unified communication platforms
  • Equipment maintenance and support

✅ Certification & Product Listing

Get your telecom products certified and listed on trusted industry portals. We facilitate the entire certification process, ensuring compliance with national and international standards.

  • TEC (Telecom Engineering Centre) certification
